How it works

The idea is simple. The details make it trustworthy.

  1. 1

    Students enroll once

    Three quick selfies (front, left, right) and an optional 5-second voice sample. Each becomes a list of numbers, a template. The photos themselves are thrown away.

  2. 2

    They join with a QR

    The teacher shares a QR code or a 6-character join code. Codes can be paused or regenerated at any time.

  3. 3

    Snap or record the class

    Upload 1–3 classroom photos, or record students saying “present”. Every face or voice is compared only with students enrolled in that subject.

  4. 4

    Review, then save

    You see the photo with names drawn on it and an editable list. Tick anyone the AI missed, then save the whole class in one atomic step.

Built to be right, not just fast

One face, one student

Faces are assigned to students most-confident-first, so two faces can never both claim the same student, and a look-alike pair is flagged for review rather than guessed.

Liveness at login

Students sign in with their roll number, then a front photo and a head-turned photo. Both must match that student. A printed picture can't turn its head.

Voice roll-call

One recording is split at the pauses. Each “present” is matched to a voice profile with a similarity score you can see.

Teacher stays in charge

Adjustable strictness, a review table before saving, and corrections to any past class, each one marked as a manual edit.

No duplicate identities

A new registration is checked against every existing face, so one person can't hold two roll numbers.

Locked-down data

Row-level security on every table, passwords hashed with bcrypt, and the database key kept on the server, never sent to a browser.

Privacy by design

Biometric data deserves care. AttendSense is built around India's DPDP Act principles: consent, purpose limitation and the right to erasure.

  • Explicit consent checkbox at registration
  • Classroom photos and audio are processed in memory, never stored
  • Only templates are kept: 128 numbers per face, 256 per voice
  • Students can replace or remove their voice and face profiles
  • “Delete my account” erases templates, enrollments and all records
